## The Power of Email Automation

Email automation allows you to send the right message to the right person at the right time—without lifting a finger. Once set up, your automation workflows run 24/7, nurturing leads while you focus on growing your business.

## Essential Automation Workflows

### 1. Welcome Series

First impressions matter. A welcome series introduces new subscribers to your brand:

**Email 1 (Immediate):** Welcome and deliver any promised lead magnet
**Email 2 (Day 2):** Share your brand story and values
**Email 3 (Day 4):** Highlight your best content or products
**Email 4 (Day 7):** Soft call-to-action or special offer

### 2. Abandoned Cart Recovery

Recover lost sales with a timely reminder sequence:

**Email 1 (1 hour):** Gentle reminder with cart contents
**Email 2 (24 hours):** Address common objections
**Email 3 (72 hours):** Final reminder with urgency or discount

### 3. Re-engagement Campaign

Win back inactive subscribers before removing them:

**Email 1:** "We miss you" with recent updates
**Email 2:** Exclusive offer to come back
**Email 3:** Final notice before removal

## Building Your First Workflow in Kibamail

### Step 1: Define Your Trigger

Every automation starts with a trigger—an event that initiates the workflow:

- Subscriber joins a list
- Tag is added
- Link is clicked
- Purchase is made
- Date-based (birthday, anniversary)

### Step 2: Map Your Sequence

Before building, sketch out your workflow:

1. What's the goal of this automation?
2. How many emails will you send?
3. What's the timing between each email?
4. Are there any decision points (if/then logic)?

### Step 3: Create Your Emails

Write compelling copy for each email in your sequence. Remember:

- Each email should have a single clear purpose
- Use personalization tokens for relevance
- Include a clear call-to-action

### Step 4: Add Conditions and Delays

Use Kibamail's visual workflow builder to add:

- **Delays:** Wait 2 days, wait until specific time
- **Conditions:** If opened previous email, if clicked link
- **Actions:** Add tag, update field, move to list

## Advanced Automation Strategies

### Behavioral Branching

Create different paths based on subscriber actions:

```
Welcome Email →
  If opened → Continue nurture sequence
  If not opened → Send re-engagement email
```

### Lead Scoring

Automatically score leads based on engagement:

- Opens email: +1 point
- Clicks link: +3 points
- Visits pricing page: +10 points
- Downloads resource: +5 points

When a lead reaches a threshold, trigger a sales notification or move them to a different sequence.

### Dynamic Content

Personalize email content based on subscriber data:

- Show different products based on past purchases
- Adjust messaging based on industry or role
- Customize offers based on engagement level

## Measuring Automation Success

Track these metrics for each workflow:

- **Completion rate:** Percentage who receive all emails
- **Conversion rate:** Percentage who take desired action
- **Revenue attributed:** Sales generated by the workflow
- **Unsubscribe rate:** Keep this low to ensure relevance

## Common Automation Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Over-Automating

Not everything needs automation. Personal touches still matter for high-value interactions.

### 2. Set and Forget

Review your automations quarterly. Update outdated content, test new approaches, and optimize based on data.

### 3. Ignoring Mobile

Most emails are read on mobile. Ensure your automated emails look great on all devices.

### 4. Too Many Emails Too Fast

Space out your emails. Nobody wants five emails in three days, no matter how valuable they are.
