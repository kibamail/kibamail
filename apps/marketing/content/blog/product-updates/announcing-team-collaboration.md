## Built for Teams

Email marketing is rarely a solo effort. Designers create templates, copywriters craft messages, marketers set up campaigns, and managers review everything before it goes out.

Today, we're launching a suite of collaboration features designed to make teamwork effortless.

## What's New

### Shared Workspaces

Create dedicated workspaces for different projects, clients, or teams. Each workspace has its own:

- Subscriber lists and segments
- Email templates and campaigns
- Automation workflows
- Analytics and reports

Switch between workspaces instantly without logging in and out.

### Role-Based Permissions

Control who can do what with granular permissions:

- **Admin** - Full access to all features and settings
- **Editor** - Create and edit content, but can't send
- **Reviewer** - Comment and approve, but can't edit
- **Analyst** - View-only access to reports and analytics

### Real-Time Collaboration

Work on emails together in real-time:

- See who else is viewing or editing
- Live cursors show where teammates are working
- Comments and mentions for async feedback
- Version history to track changes

### Approval Workflows

Set up custom approval processes:

1. Designer creates email
2. Copywriter reviews text
3. Manager gives final approval
4. Campaign goes live

Emails can't be sent until all approvals are collected.

## Getting Started with Teams

### Invite Your Team

Navigate to Settings → Team Members to invite colleagues. They'll receive an email invitation to join your account.

### Set Up Workspaces

Create workspaces for different projects or clients. Assign team members to relevant workspaces based on their roles.

### Configure Permissions

Customize permissions for each team member. You can set account-level roles and workspace-specific permissions.

## Pricing

Team collaboration features are available on our Team and Enterprise plans:

- **Team Plan** - Up to 10 team members, 3 workspaces
- **Enterprise Plan** - Unlimited team members and workspaces, plus SSO and advanced security

Existing customers on these plans will see the new features in their dashboard today.
